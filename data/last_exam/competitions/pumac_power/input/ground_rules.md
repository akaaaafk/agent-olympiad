# Competition rules

You are an eligible high-school contestant on a fixed eight-person PUMaC Power team. You are under 20 and not a full-time post-secondary student before the contest date. Power-Only teams are also exactly eight people; individuals may not enter Power-Only. All eight of you may collaborate. The Power Round is released about a week before its due time, which is printed on the test; as of 2023 you submit online at that printed time, not on competition day. This session's turn budget does not reproduce that week. Use no books, internet, or people outside the eight unless the test says otherwise. Computational aids are only those the year's Power packet allows—default deny—and Individual or Team no-calculator rules do not apply here. If this briefing conflicts with the packet, follow the packet. Write full justifications; bare answers earn no credit unless the test says so. You may cite an earlier part you could not prove. Handwrite or type on one side of the page; label every page with team number and problem number; never print the team name. Submit exactly once; the first set received is graded. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
