# Competition rules

MIT Mystery Hunt. The team works through material released in stages and commits answers under the event sequence. Benchmark adaptation: the runner permits 8 to 12 active agents and defaults to 12; official roster note: The 2026 FAQ states no official team-size recommendation; 8-12 is only the runner's configured range. The configured agents may freely coordinate inside their benchmark team; they may not obtain answers from another competing team. Banned during the contest: calculators and help from other teams. Permitted: internet access and running code. Paper and pencil are always available. Submit one shared puzzle answer; Submit the team's final answers in numbered order, using exact values when required. Open-ended hunt timing; this row is one puzzle. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
