# Competition rules

Wharton Global High School Investment Competition. The team researches, authors, and defends a judged artifact. The source-recorded active team may have 4 to 6 members; the runner default is 5. Collaborate only within the team under contest rules. Permitted: internet access, calculators and running code. Conditional: generative AI is limited to cited brainstorming and live market simulator is unavailable in runner. Paper and pencil are always available. Submit one shared investment report; Submit clearly separated Investment Policy Statement and Comprehensive Final Report sections covering client objectives, risk tolerance, strategy, evidence, portfolio decisions, and citations. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
