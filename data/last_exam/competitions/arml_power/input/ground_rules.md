# Competition rules

This sitting is a team Power proof packet. It is not a complete current ARML Power Contest administrivia package: the dedicated Rules page is Under Construction, and you must not invent a sit clock, official roster, or computational-aid exception. Where published national ARML Power rules are the only available Power constraints, the packet is worth 50 points and the team files exactly one solution; extra packets are all scored and the lowest is kept. Write the justifications the verbs require. Calculators and electronic devices are banned; paper and pencil only; no internet, books, or outside help. Do not treat a list of bare answers as a complete Power packet. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
