Thirteen at Dinner

 
 

Excerpt from the Journal of Alexander von Humboldt:

During the festival, the Aztecs held dinners on six consecutive nights, at a single large table in the plaza near the Moon Pyramid, each attended by thirteen of their most celebrated members.
