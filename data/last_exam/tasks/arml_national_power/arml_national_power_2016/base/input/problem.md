One way to ﬁx this problem is to deﬁne the measure of an angle analogously to the way the radian measure of an angle is deﬁned in Euclidean geometry, namely, as a quotient. The numerator is the length of the arc that the angle intercepts on a circle centered at the angle’s vertex. The denominator is the radius of that circle.
