The first letters of the titles (after "The Case of [the]") spell out WRITE HOW HE SOLVED WHO GRABBED PEACH. So, your team has to write a short Encyclopedia Brown–style mystery solution explaining how he was able to identify the guilty party in Peach's kidnapping. Once you do that, you get given the answer to the puzzle, CUL DE SAC.
