# Competition rules

DebateBench (WUDC / BP). The team prepares a case, presents it, and responds to judges or opponents. Mixed or unresolved rulesets: the runner permits 8 to 8 active agents and defaults to 8. Eight participants form four private two-speaker coalitions. Each speaker may prepare only with their partner; cross-coalition sharing is forbidden. Banned during the contest: internet access, calculators and running code. Paper and pencil are always available. Work only from the materials provided with the problem. Submit one shared oral speech; Submit eight labeled speeches in official order: PM, LO, DPM, DLO, MG, MO, GW, OW. Preserve coalition privacy and identify POIs separately. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
