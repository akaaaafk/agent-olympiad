Sunday Noon, Trader Levi
Jeremiah's intelligence is a surprise, coming from such a short, hirsute body. He was sent in to town for supplies- moonshine and dynamite for the most part as usual- but at each stop he was held up by someone with a problem. To him, they're elementary, but most people up here haven't made it past the third grade, what with being eaten by bears or dropping out to dig for gold...

2004 MIT Mystery Hunt
