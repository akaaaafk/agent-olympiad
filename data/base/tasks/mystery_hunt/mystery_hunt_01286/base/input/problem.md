# Dr Ox's Experiment

Open `puzzle.html` for the original local attachment links. The original PDF/FLV files are the inputs; text previews do not replace them.

Archive limitation: The original PDF and/or video attachments, ordering and player images are preserved byte-for-byte. Obsolete player scripts are removed and original links point to local files. FLV tag boundaries are complete, but every original video has metadata filesize 38 bytes greater than its actual length. This discrepancy and original 0x0d header flags are preserved, not repaired or interpreted as proof of codec validity; decoding, listening and viewing are not certified.
