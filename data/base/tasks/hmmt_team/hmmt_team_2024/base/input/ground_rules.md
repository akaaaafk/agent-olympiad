# Competition rules

Work as one HMMT team for 60 minutes on the 10-problem Team Round. November uses short answers and a 4–6 person team; February uses proofs, permits partial credit, and uses a 6–8 person team. Use the season attached to the benchmark row. Books, notes, calculators, drawing aids, phones, computers, online sources, and outside help are forbidden. Submit one shared numbered answer or proof packet.
