# Competition rules

World Robot Olympiad. The task represents a physical robotics event through design, rules, and program text. The source-recorded active team may have 2 to 3 members; the runner default is 3; official roster note: A 2026 RoboMission team consists of two or three students and is guided by a coach. The two or three student members design, build, and program the robot; a coach may guide learning but may not build or program it for the team. Banned during the contest: internet access, calculators and running code. Paper and pencil are always available. Work only from the materials provided with the problem. Submit one shared inspected robot program and scored field attempt; Submit the program and a structured design/run analysis; label simulated or hypothetical behavior and do not report an unobserved physical score. Treat unexposed physical, oral, live-opponent, judge, timing, or environment mechanisms as unavailable rather than simulated facts.
