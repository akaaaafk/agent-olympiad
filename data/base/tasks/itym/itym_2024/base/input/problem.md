4. Guessing Bracket Sequences
A balanced bracket sequence is a string consisting of only brackets, such that this sequence,
when inserted certain numbers and mathematical operations, gives a valid mathematical ex-
pression. For instance(())() is a balanced bracket sequence, but())( is not.
Maksim is playing the following game. Letk be a positive integer. The goal is to guess a
balanced bracket sequence, if it is known that
a) the length of the sequence is2k;
b) the length of the sequence is at most2k (note that if Maksim asks the host to reveal a
bracket at a position where there isn’t any, the host replies with “no bracket”).
Each move Maksim can ask the host to reveal the bracket at any position. Maksim wins when
he correctly guesses the bracket sequence.
1. Let M (k) be the minimal number of moves with which Maksim can guarantee a win
regardless of the initial sequence. FindM (k) or estimate it.
2. Now, assume that with every bracket the host also reveals the bracket that comes in pair
with it. Let L(k) be the minimal number of moves with which Maksim can guarantee a win
regardless of the initial sequence. FindL(k) or estimate it.
3. If the bracket sequence is not necessarily balanced, how many moves, in terms ofk, does

7. Compositions of Polynomials

1. Suppose P (x) has rational coeﬃcients and degree at least two andP (P (x)) has integer
coeﬃcients.
a) Is it true thatP (x) also has integer coeﬃcients?
b) What if we impose the additional condition thatP (0) is an integer?
2. a) Determineallpairs (a,b )ofpositiveintegerswiththefollowingproperty: anypolynomial
P (x) with rational coeﬃcients, such thatPa(x) andPb(x) have integer coeﬃcients, also
has integer coeﬃcients.

at least 1000?

integer coeﬃcients?
4. Suppose P (x) and Q(x) are two polynomials with the same degree and have rational
coeﬃcients butP (Q(x)) and Q(P (x)) have integer coeﬃcients. Is it always true that at least
one ofP (x) and Q(x) has integer coeﬃcients?
5. We call a monic polynomialP (x), of degree at least 4, monic decomposable over R if
P (x) = A(B(x)) for some monic polynomialsA(x),B (x)∈R[x] of degree at least2.
a) Prove that ifP (x)∈Z[x] is monic decomposable overC, then it is also monic decom-

coeﬃcients ofQ(x) is 1. Suppose thatP (x) has complex coeﬃcients andP (Q(x)) has integer
coeﬃcients. Prove thatP (x) also has integer coeﬃcients.
