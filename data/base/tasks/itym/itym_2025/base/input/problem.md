irreducible over Z if there do not exist non-constant polynomialsg(x) and h(x) with integer
coeﬃcients such thatf(x) =g(x)h(x).
Let n ≥2 and a ̸= 0 be integers. We are interested in some polynomials of the form
fn,a(x) =xn−x +a.

irreducible over Z.
