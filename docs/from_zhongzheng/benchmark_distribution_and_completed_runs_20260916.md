## Scope and counting rules

- Canonical inventory: active entries in `data/benchmarks/index.json`; archived or unindexed benchmark directories are excluded.
- Years covered means distinct years represented in this local collection, not the total years a competition has existed.
- Records use mixed units: questions, full papers, cases, and task packets. Record totals are not a total number of individual questions.
- An annual edition, a seasonal event, a round packet, and an age-group session are different units; the table states the available unit. Distinct-year counts alone are not used to claim official edition counts.
- Scoring parts are not automatically independent questions. Question counts are not multiplied by the three settings.
- Catalog inclusion does not imply evaluation readiness. `not_ready` records remain explicitly marked.

## Mini scoring (retained 2026-09-17 scope; PDF delivery revalidated)

How the independent mini catalog grades a submitted answer. Source of truth: each record's `evaluation` in `data/mini_data/benchmarks/<track>/benchmark.json`, plus the bound rubric under `data/mini_data/rubrics/`. Rule-card `evaluation.json` files are defaults only.

Task score, rule-compliance counts, and collaboration scores are reported separately. Collaboration (CS / IHS) does not overwrite the task score. `single_agent` skips CS only. LLM rubric scores set `official_score_equivalent=false`. Criteria marked `observable: false` (live opposition, oral, red team, instruments) are dropped before judging, so the reported `max_score` can be smaller than the official sheet.

After `submit`, `env.grade_submission()` tries gold matching or a programming judge. Open-ended records then go through `apply_registered_judge()` / `contest_task_judge.py`. HMMT Team uses `gold_else_llm_v1`: verified short answers are exact gold; remaining proof parts use the official-reference LLM rubric.
